# Privacy Policy — RouteShare

_Last updated: 2026-07-23_

RouteShare ("the app") is a GPS route-recording and navigation-guidance app.
This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled. In plain
terms: **your data stays on your device — RouteShare has no servers and no user
accounts.**

## Who we are

RouteShare is developed by Gopi Kramadhati.
Contact: **gopikramadhati@gmail.com**

## What data the app uses

**Location (GPS).** The app accesses your device's precise location so it can:

- record a route as you travel and mark stops along it;
- follow a saved route and alert you as you approach each stop;
- provide "Guided Drive," which hands each leg of a route to Google Maps.

Location is accessed only while you are actively recording, following, or
running a guided drive. When recording or following, a foreground-service
notification is shown so you know location is in use, and so tracking continues
if the screen turns off.

**No other personal data is collected.** The app does not ask for your name,
email, phone number, contacts, photos, or any account sign-in.

## Where your data is stored

All routes you record — including their coordinates and stops — are stored
**locally on your device**. RouteShare does **not** upload your routes or
location to any server, and the developer cannot see them.

## Sharing is user-initiated

You can choose to share a route (as a file, a KML file, or a Google Maps link)
using your device's standard share options. This only happens when you tap
"share" and pick a destination (e.g. WhatsApp, Gmail, Google Drive). RouteShare
does not share anything automatically.

## Third-party services

- **Google Maps.** The app shows maps using the Google Maps SDK and opens the
  Google Maps app for turn-by-turn navigation. Your use of Google Maps is
  governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

RouteShare does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking
SDKs.

## Permissions the app requests

- **Location** — to record and follow routes (see above).
- **Notifications** — to show the tracking/guided-drive status.
- **Display over other apps** (Android) — to show the small "guided drive"
  control card on top of Google Maps while navigating.

You can revoke any permission in your device settings; some features will stop
working without them.

## Children

RouteShare is a general-purpose navigation tool and is not directed at children
under 13.

## Data deletion

Because routes are stored only on your device, you can delete them at any time
from within the app (delete a saved route), or by uninstalling the app, which
removes all stored routes.
